Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Your data. Your control.

JoSo Calorie Tracker is designed to keep personal JoSo tracking data under the user's control. JoSo does not operate a JoSo cloud account or cloud database that stores your personal food diary, calorie history, Cycle records or Injection Tracking records for later retrieval by us.

Information stored on your device

Information you enter into JoSo, including food and hydration records, preferences, saved meals, private health-tracking records and other app history, is primarily stored locally on your device.

Backups and deletion

JoSo provides an export feature that lets you create a backup file and choose where to save it. You are responsible for retaining and protecting exported backups. If JoSo is deleted, your device is lost or erased, or local app data is otherwise removed without a usable backup, we cannot retrieve or restore that local information because we do not hold a server-side copy.

Apple Health and device services

Where you choose to enable supported Apple Health features, JoSo may request permission to read selected information through Apple's HealthKit framework. Access is controlled by iOS permissions. JoSo also uses device capabilities such as notifications, camera access and Face ID/device authentication only where required for features you choose to use.

External services

Some optional features may contact third-party services to provide functionality, for example product information lookups or links to external websites. Those services may process technical information according to their own privacy practices. JoSo does not sell your personal JoSo tracking data.

Private health features

Cycle and Injection Tracking are optional. Where enabled, their JoSo records are stored locally as part of the app's private health functionality and can use JoSo's Face ID/device-authentication protection.

Contact

For privacy questions, use our support page.
