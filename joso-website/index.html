Your data. Your control.

JoSo is designed without a JoSo cloud account for your personal tracking records. Your JoSo data is stored locally on your device, so we cannot view or retrieve your local diary and private tracking records.

You can export a backup from the app and choose where to keep it. If the app or device is deleted or erased without a usable backup, JoSo cannot recover that local data for you.
